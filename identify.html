Know your material

Plastic types guide

Resin codes identify the plastic material; they do not guarantee that it is accepted for recycling locally.

01PET

Polyethylene terephthalate

Often used for drink bottles and food containers.

Before recycling: Empty and rinse it. Check whether your local programme accepts it.

02HDPE

High-density polyethylene

Often used for milk, detergent and shampoo containers.

Before recycling: Empty and rinse containers, then check local acceptance.

04LDPE

Low-density polyethylene

Often used for bags, wraps and flexible films.

Before recycling: Keep bags and film out of household bins; look for a participating store drop-off.

05PP

Polypropylene

Often used for tubs, caps and some food containers.

Before recycling: Check your local programme—acceptance varies by area and item.

06PS

Polystyrene

Often used for foam packaging, cups and some food trays.

Before recycling: Few local programmes accept foam; check for a suitable drop-off before bringing it.

i Recycling rules depend on your location and the specific item. When in doubt, check your local provider to avoid contaminating a recycling load.
